The board

The value model — fitted, tested, and shown

log(mcap) = 3.2254 + 0.7002·log(TVL) + 0.8507·log(1+growth) + 0.1129·log(age) − 0.0862·log(chains)

Fitted by Huber-robust regression on the 106 comparable live-token protocols (market cap between 0.01× and 3× TVL). In-sample R² 0.50. The other 22 live-token protocols fall outside that scope — their tokens trade far above or below TVL (a drained legacy pool, a chain token, a prediction market) — and are named in the coverage table below, not hidden.

  • Leave-one-out (n=106): median error ×3.02, 28% of values within 2×, Spearman 0.62.
  • Walk-forward, cutoff 2024-01-01 (train 38, test 43): R² 0.09, median error ×3.05, 40% within 2×, Spearman 0.67.
  • Walk-forward, cutoff 2024-07-01 (train 46, test 35): R² 0.11, median error ×3.31, 34% within 2×, Spearman 0.72.
  • Rank ranges from 200 refits: every rank below is shown with its 5th–95th percentile range across 200 bootstrap refits of the model — a rank of #3 (2–5) is solid, a rank of #80 (40–150) is a coin flip. Launch values are noisy; the board says so instead of pretending otherwise.

Scope: the model prices protocols whose token value still tracks TVL. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to −60%…+200% (null → 0); age is days since DefiLlama began tracking (unknown → 730 days); chains is the count of chains. No protocol name is ever special-cased — one function scores every row, and the detail view recomputes each value in this page so you can check it.

Every one of the 758 protocols, accounted for

758 = 198 ranked (strict pre-token) + 128 live-token with a market cap (106 in the fit, 22 out of scope) + 144 not ranked that record no token (exchanges, bridges, staking receipts, versioned pools) + 288 token-recorded with no market cap (272 null, 15 zero, 1 negative record).

How to read this board

  • Implied value is this entry's own claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It is not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued.
  • Rank range is the 5th–95th percentile of the rank across 200 refits. Wide ranges mean the model cannot tell those protocols apart — farm the top of a range, not a single rank.
  • Farm plans are costed per protocol: the deposit is sized to its TVL ($55–$275), the gas per step comes from its first-listed chain, and the hold is 30–60 days by age. Fees are the gas across the plan's transactions; the deposit is capital at risk, not a fee.
  • Live-token protocols never take a pre-token rank. They are shown with the market's own value, as the comparables the model learned from.
